# Genre Research Sheet — Column Guide

Hey! This doc explains every column in the Google Sheet and how to fill it. At the bottom there's a **fully filled example** you can reference. For each video, watch it **3 times:**

1. **First watch:** Experience it normally — note your gut reaction
2. **Second watch:** Pause and transcript the full script, fill info columns
3. **Third watch:** Focus on audio, pacing, pauses, SFX

**Time per video: ~20 minutes**

## Section 3: 🪝 HOOK & STRUCTURE (Columns O–X)

How the script is built — the opening, middle, and ending.

| Column | What To Write | How To Think About It |
|---|---|---|
| **Hook (First Sentence)** | The very first sentence of the script | What do they say to grab your attention? |
| **Hook Type** | Dropdown — classify what KIND of hook it is | Is it a question? A shocking fact? A mystery? A "what if"? Can be multiple like `question + time_reference` |
| **Hook Emotional Trigger** | Dropdown — what FEELING does the hook create? | Does it make you curious? Scared? Shocked? Disbelieving? |
| **Hook Speed (sec)** | How many seconds until the first pause or period | Time from start to first full stop or natural pause |
| **Opening Words (first 5)** | The first 5 words of the video | `Did you know that in` |
| **Body Sentence Count** | Number of sentences between hook and ending | Just the middle sentences (not first, not last) |
| **Has Twist/Reveal?** | Is there a surprising moment? | A twist doesn't have to be a "plot twist" — it's any moment that surprises you |
| **Twist Line** | Write the actual twist sentence | `she can hear you` |
| **Ending Type** | Dropdown — how does it end? | Does it leave you hanging? Ask a question? Circle back to the start? |
| **Last Sentence** | Write the final sentence exactly | `And the house? It's still for sale.` |

## Section 4: 📊 SCRIPT ANALYSIS (Columns Y–AH)

Deeper analysis of HOW the script is written.

| Column | What To Write | Why It Matters |
|---|---|---|
| **Tense Used** | Present, past, or mixed? | Scary stories often use past. Motivation uses present ("You are...") |
| **POV / Person** | Who is "speaking"? | `you_2nd_person` = "You wake up at 3AM..." / `narrator` = "A family discovered..." |
| **Rhetorical Questions Count** | How many questions are asked that aren't meant to be answered? | "But what if they came back?" = 1 rhetorical question |
| **Power Words** | List ALL emotionally charged words | Any word that makes you FEEL something: "terrifying", "sealed", "vanished", "destroyed", "ancient" |
| **Emphasis Words** | Words the narrator says LOUDER or SLOWER | Listen carefully — which words does the voice stress? These become our caption highlights |
| **Narrative Technique** | Dropdown — the storytelling method used | `escalating_reveal` = each sentence reveals more / `list_format` = "5 facts about..." / `mystery_solve` = sets up mystery then explains |
| **Emotional Arc** | Describe the emotional journey from start to end | `curiosity → unease → dread → shock → lingering fear` |
| **Has Numbers/Stats?** | Any numbers, years, statistics mentioned? | Numbers make content feel factual and real: "1973", "40 years", "3 days" |
| **Has Direct Address?** | Does the narrator talk directly TO you? | "YOU won't believe this" = yes. Narrating a story about "they" = no |
| **Banned Phrases Found** | Any overused generic phrases? | Things like "Scientists say...", "According to studies..." feel robotic. Note if they appear |

## Section 5: ⏱️ SCRIPT PACING (Columns AI–AT)

How the rhythm and flow of the script works. This is what separates amateur from professional writing.

| Column | What To Write | Why It Matters |
|---|---|---|
| **Avg Sentence Length (words)** | Total words ÷ total sentences | Helps us set our word-per-sentence target per genre |
| **Shortest Sentence** | Write the shortest sentence + its word count | Short sentences = IMPACT. "The bed was still made." (5 words) punches hard |
| **Longest Sentence** | Write the longest sentence + its word count | Long sentences build context and setting |
| **Short:Long Sentence Ratio** | Count short (≤8 words) vs long (>8 words) | `3:4` means 3 short, 4 long. Viral videos often mix both |
| **Sentence Length Pattern** | Dropdown — describe the rhythm | `long_then_short_punch` = setup with long sentences then BAM short sentence for impact |
| **Info Density** | How many new facts/details per sentence? | `1.0` = every sentence adds something new (good). Low density = filler (bad) |
| **Surprise/Twist Count** | How many "wow" or unexpected moments? | Even in a 50-sec video, 2-3 surprises keeps attention |
| **Has CTA?** | Is there any call-to-action? | "Subscribe for more" / "Comment if..." / "Follow for part 2" |
| **CTA Placement** | Where is the CTA? | `end` is most common. `start` is rare but some genres do it |
| **Repetition/Callback?** | Any words or phrases that repeat intentionally? | In our example: "still" repeats 3 times — still made, still on floor, still for sale. This is deliberate. |
| **Sensory Language Used** | What senses does the script activate? | `visual:` bed, toys, scratched / `auditory:` hear. More senses = more immersive |

## Section 6: 🎙️ VOICE & PACING (Columns AU–BA)

How the narrator SOUNDS. You need to LISTEN carefully on the 3rd watch.

| Column | What To Write | How To Think About It |
|---|---|---|
| **Voice Gender** | Male, female, or AI-neutral | Is it clearly gendered? Some AI voices are harder to tell |
| **Voice Tone** | Dropdown — the overall vibe of the voice | `deep_authoritative` = sounds like a news anchor / `whispery_dramatic` = sounds like telling a ghost story |
| **Speaking Speed** | Dropdown — slow/medium/fast | Slow = <2.5 words/sec, Medium = 2.5-3.5, Fast = >3.5 |
| **Pause After Hook (ms)** | Is there a silence after the first sentence? Estimate in milliseconds | Count "one-Mississippi" — 500ms = half a beat pause |
| **Pause Before Reveal (ms)** | Is there a pause just before the big twist? | Usually the biggest pause in the video. "And on the wall... [PAUSE] ...she can hear you" |
| **Other Notable Pauses** | Where else does the narrator pause? | Between sentences? After dramatic words? Note the location |
| **Words Spoken Louder/Slower** | Which words get extra emphasis vocally? | The narrator will slow down or get louder on key words. These words become our caption highlight words |

## Section 7: 🖼️ VISUALS (Columns BB–BP)

What you SEE on screen.

| Column | What To Write | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Layout Type** | Dropdown — what's on screen? | `split_screen` = images on top + gameplay bottom / `full_images` = images fill whole screen |
| **Face Visible?** | Is a person's face shown? | Important for deciding our video layout |
| **Image Count** | How many different images appear? | Count each distinct image change |
| **Image Change Timing** | How often do images switch? | "Every 8-12 seconds" or "synced to each sentence" |
| **Transition Type** | Dropdown — how do images change? | `cut` = instant switch / `dissolve` = fade into each other / `zoom` = zooms into next |
| **Image Style** | Dropdown — what do the images LOOK like? | `dark_moody` for horror / `bright_colorful` for motivation |
| **Ken Burns Effect?** | Do images slowly zoom/pan? | If the image isn't static but slowly moves, that's Ken Burns |
| **Images Match Words?** | Do the images relate to what's being said? | `perfectly_synced` = image appears right when the word is said |
| **Image Descriptions** | Describe EACH image briefly, numbered | `1. Old house (hook) \| 2. Brick wall (sealed room) \| 3. Bedroom (child's room)` |
| **Gameplay Type** | If there's gameplay, which game? | Minecraft parkour, Subway Surfers, etc. or "none" |
| **Gameplay Position** | Where is the gameplay on screen? | Usually bottom half for split-screen |
| **Color Grading / Filter** | What's the overall color feel? | `cold_desaturated` = dark/blue / `warm` = orange/cozy |
| **Text Overlays** | Any extra text on screen besides captions? | Title cards, fact labels, arrows pointing at things |

## Section 8: 📝 CAPTIONS (Columns BQ–BW)

The text that appears synced to the narration.

| Column | What To Write | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Caption Style** | Dropdown — how do captions appear? | `word_at_a_time` = 2-3 words pop on/off / `karaoke_wipe` = sentence fills with color as spoken |
| **Caption Font** | What does the font look like? | Bold sans-serif? Serif? Handwritten? If you recognize it: "Montserrat Black" |
| **Caption Primary Color** | Main color of the text | Usually white |
| **Caption Highlight Color** | Color used for emphasized words | Red, yellow, or green are common |
| **Caption Position** | Where on screen are captions? | `center` is most common for Shorts |
| **Caption Border/Glow?** | Is there an outline or glow around text? | Helps readability — "black 3px border" or "red glow on emphasis" |
| **Words Per Caption Pop** | How many words appear at once? | 1 word? 2-3 words? A whole sentence? |

## Section 9: 🎵 AUDIO (Columns BX–CH)

Background music and sound effects. Listen with headphones for best results.

| Column | What To Write | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Has Background Music?** | Is there music playing under the voice? | Sometimes it's very subtle |
| **Music Mood** | Dropdown — what does the music feel like? | `eerie` / `dramatic` / `upbeat` / `chill` |
| **Music Volume Level** | How loud is it relative to the voice? | `barely_audible` = you barely notice it / `loud` = clearly present |
| **Music Fades?** | Does music fade in at start or fade out at end? | Most professional videos fade |
| **Music Changes Mid-Video?** | Does the music change or build during the video? | Some videos have the music intensity build toward the twist |
| **Has Sound Effects?** | Any non-music audio effects? | Booms, whooshes, stingers, ambient sounds |
| **SFX at Hook** | What sound plays at/near the first sentence? | `bass hit` / `whoosh` / `none` |
| **SFX at Reveal/Twist** | What sound plays at the twist moment? | `horror stinger` / `dramatic boom` / `none` |
| **SFX at Ending** | What sound plays at the end? | `deep drone` / `suspense build` / `none` |
| **Other SFX Notes** | Any other sound effects throughout? | Ambient sounds, scratching, wind, etc. |
| **Audio Clarity** | How clean is the audio overall? | `crystal_clear` = professional / `slightly_noisy` = has artifacts |

## Section 11: 🔥 VIRALITY SIGNALS (Columns CQ–CZ)

WHY this video went viral. This is the analysis section.

| Column | What To Write | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Like-to-View Ratio (%)** | Auto-calculated — likes ÷ views × 100 | Above 4% = excellent engagement |
| **Comment-to-View Ratio (%)** | Auto-calculated — comments ÷ views × 100 | Above 0.05% = good discussion |
| **Estimated Save/Share Level** | How likely are people to share or save this? | `very_high` = people will definitely tag friends |
| **Why Would Someone Share This?** | What specific moment makes someone send this to a friend? | "The twist 'she can hear you' — people tag friends to scare them" |
| **Re-Watch Value** | Would someone watch this twice? | `high` = there are details you catch on rewatch |
| **Controversy/Debate Potential** | Does this spark arguments in comments? | Debate = more comments = algorithm boost |
| **Trend Alignment** | Is this riding a current trend or is it evergreen? | `none_evergreen` = works anytime / `riding_current_trend` = topical |
| **First Frame: Scroll-Stop?** | Would the very first frame make you stop scrolling? | Describe what you see in frame 1 |
| **First Frame: Motion?** | Is there movement in the first frame? | `yes_ken_burns_zoom` / `yes_gameplay_motion` / `no_static` |
| **Would YOU Stop Scrolling?** | Honest answer — would YOU stop? | Your honest gut reaction matters |

## Section 12: 💬 ENGAGEMENT (Columns DA–DG)

What's happening in the comments — this tells us what resonates.

| Column | What To Write | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Top Comment Theme** | What are most comments about? | "People sharing their own creepy house stories" |
| **Comment Sentiment** | Dropdown — overall feeling in comments | `engaged_scared` / `engaged_debate` / `asking_for_more` |
| **Do Comments Add Stories?** | Are viewers sharing their own related experiences? | This is a strong engagement signal |
| **Creator Replies in Comments?** | Does the creator interact? | Pinned comment? Replies to viewers? |
| **Likely Share Trigger** | What SPECIFIC moment makes someone share? | "The 'she can hear you' twist — people tag friends" |
| **Retention Hook** | What keeps you watching through the MIDDLE? | "Escalating mystery — each sentence more disturbing" |
| **Where Would You Stop Watching?** | At what point (if any) did you feel bored or tempted to scroll? | If the answer is "nowhere" — that's a sign of great pacing |

## Section 14: ⭐ SCORES (Columns DK–DU)

Your personal ratings. Be honest — this helps us figure out what makes a 9/10 vs a 5/10.

| Column | What To Rate | Scoring Guide |
|---|---|---|
| **Score: Hook (1-10)** | How strong is the opening? | 9-10: Impossible not to keep watching. 5-6: Decent. 1-3: Boring |
| **Score: Script (1-10)** | How well-written is the content? | Does every sentence earn its place? Any filler? |
| **Score: Pacing (1-10)** | Does the rhythm feel right? | Too fast? Too slow? Perfect tension? |
| **Score: Visual (1-10)** | How good do the visuals look? | Are images relevant? Good quality? Good transitions? |
| **Score: Audio (1-10)** | How does everything sound? | Clear voice? Good music? Well-placed SFX? |
| **Score: Shareability (1-10)** | Would people share this? | Does it have a share-worthy moment? |
| **Score: Overall (1-10)** | Your total impression | How good is this as a YouTube Short overall? |
| **Why It Worked** | Write 2-3 sentences on WHY this video is successful | Focus on the specific things that make it engaging |
| **What To Improve** | What would make this video even better? | Be constructive — what's the one weak spot? |
| **Usable as Few-Shot Example?** | Can we use this script to teach our AI? | `yes` = high quality, representative of the genre |
| **Additional Notes** | Anything else worth noting | Patterns, observations, connections to other videos |

## Quick Tips

- **If a column doesn't apply**, write `none` or `N/A` — don't leave it blank
- **Dropdowns:** Click the cell to see options — saves you from typing
- **Be specific, not vague:** "good music" ❌ → "eerie low drone that builds at reveal" ✅
- **The script column is KING** — spend the most time getting this right
- **15 top videos per genre + 5 failures** — the failures teach us what NOT to do
- **Score honestly** — a 6/10 is fine! We need the range to spot patterns
